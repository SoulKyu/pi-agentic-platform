- PF-8 (T16a, T16b): J20's plumbing is in scope: `startPap`/`startDaemon` gain an extra-args option (`papArgs`, e.g. `["--insecure-http"]`); `pap.origin`, the cookie URL and `setupOwner`'s origin come from `PAP_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` when set; the task documents how container-mode browsers (`PAP_E2E_WS`) reach the host's loopback proxy. `scripts/e2e/daemon.ts` and `scripts/smoke-lib.ts` join the Conflict scan.
- PF-9 (T14, T15): T14 adds `diagram: string` to `RichReplies.labels` and to the labels builder in `reply-markdown.ts`, with its catalog entry; T15 only consumes it.
- PF-10 (T10): `grantOf("artifact", …)` and `previews.artifact` bind unconditionally; the service side (registry, tunnels) is optional. One int case: with no VM stack, `previews.artifact` still mints and the listener serves it; `previews.list` is empty and `previews.open` answers `box_stopped` or `unavailable`.
