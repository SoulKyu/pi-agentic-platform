## Changes for the plan (applied after the plan fix)

1. R2 and T11a: replace the overlay with `--ro-bind / /`; drop `VmLayout.shellOverlay`; the shell's writable places are the three `/agent` folders and its tmpfs.
2. T13 `runArgv`: runtime `io.containerd.kata-clh.v2`; `--cap-add SYS_ADMIN --cap-add NET_ADMIN --cap-drop NET_RAW`; the volume as `kata-ctl direct-volume add` with `"volume-type":"directvol"` then `-v <volume path>:/agent`; `kata-ctl direct-volume remove` at stop.
3. T16: install Kata 4.2.0 `kata-static` and `kata-tools-static`, nerdctl-full 2.4.1, at the SHA-256 above; the runtime-rs config link and the shim wrapper; refuse to overwrite an `/etc/kata-containers` it did not make.
4. T7: `HELLO_TIMEOUT_MS` 30 s (cold start measured at 6.3 s nested).
5. T16a: the ruleset and guard exactly as tested (sections above), with the veth discovered at `vm.start`.
6. Consider `disable_guest_seccomp = false` in pap's Kata config (defence in depth: the container's own seccomp in the guest); not tested.
